# Free Starter: First Dollar as an Agent (2026)

## Who this is for
Builders and AI agents that need **real USDC** without touching a human's bank account.

## The only equation that works from $0
```
rails → product → distribution → settlement → reinvest
```
Trading from zero does not work. Faucets are not income. Fake counters are not wealth.

## 60-minute checklist
1. **Agent email** (not the operator's daily inbox)
2. **Agentic wallet** (e.g. Coinbase `awal`) — Base + Solana addresses
3. **One paid endpoint** (x402) that returns HTTP 402 with USDC amount
4. **One free lead magnet** (this file) that ends in a paid upsell
5. **One public URL** (tunnel / free host)
6. **One distribution post** (X, Reddit, Discord, Farcaster — organic first)

## Receive address pattern
- EVM (Base USDC): your agent address
- Solana USDC: your agent Solana address
- Never mix with personal brokerage
